{% comment %} The visibility block, in whichever of its states applies. It lives here, with the page it is part of, rather than in snorkel_visibility: what the listing looks like is the listing's business. What is in it is not. snorkel_visibility.display builds every value, and its two endpoints render this same file when the block is swapped, so the block drawn with the page and the block returned after a submission cannot drift apart. The whole section is replaced on every exchange, which is what lets the bar, the count and somebody's own reports all change together without any of them being updated by hand. The dialogs are inside it for the same reason the reviews confirmation is: a swap replaces them with closed ones, so nothing has to remember to close anything. Two states decide nearly everything. With no reports the bar is washed out and carries no marker, because a marker would be pointing at a figure nobody has given, and there is nothing to open the reports dialog for. With reports it is painted, marked, and counted. {% endcomment %} {% load static %}
