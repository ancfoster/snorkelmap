Editing on SnorkelMap

Every snorkelling location on SnorkelMap was written by somebody who has been in the water there, and any of it can be improved by anybody else who has.

How it works

A listing is not owned by the person who started it. If you know the car park now charges, that the steps are out at low water, or that the seals have moved along the coast, you can say so. Add what is missing, correct what has changed, and leave the rest alone.

Changes appear on the listing straight away. Every one of them is kept, with the name of whoever made it, in a history anybody can read, so nothing is lost and nothing is anonymous.

What we ask

  • Write about places you have actually snorkelled.
  • Say what you know, and leave out what you are guessing at.
  • Be careful with hazards. Somebody may get in the water on the strength of what you write.
  • Leave a note explaining anything that is not obvious.

To start editing, open a listing and use the Edit link at the top of it. You will need an account, which is free.
