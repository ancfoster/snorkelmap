SnorkelMap.com Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

1. About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how The Snorkelling Society, a charity registered in Scotland under charity number SC054133, collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use SnorkelMap.com (the “Website” or “SnorkelMap”).

The Snorkelling Society's registered address is:

The Snorkelling Society

26 Nelson Street

Edinburgh

EH3 6LJ

United Kingdom

For privacy and data-protection enquiries, please contact:

contact@snorkellingsociety.org

The Snorkelling Society is the organisation responsible for the processing of personal information described in this Privacy Policy.

We operate SnorkelMap as a not-for-profit charitable service intended to support snorkelling, marine awareness, marine conservation, education, science and the wider snorkelling community.

We do not sell users' personal information to third parties.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with the SnorkelMap Terms and Conditions.

2. The data protection laws that apply

We process personal information in accordance with applicable UK data-protection law, including the:

  • UK General Data Protection Regulation (“UK GDPR”);
  • Data Protection Act 2018;
  • Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (“PECR”); and
  • other applicable data-protection and privacy legislation as amended or replaced from time to time.

The Information Commissioner's Office (“ICO”) is the UK's independent regulator responsible for data protection and information rights.

3. What information we collect

3.1 Account information

If you create an account, we may collect:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • your username or display name;
  • your password, stored using appropriate security measures;
  • account and authentication information;
  • information associated with your account activity; and
  • information you voluntarily provide to us.

3.2 User-generated content

Depending on how you use SnorkelMap, we may collect:

  • snorkel location listings;
  • edits to snorkel location listings;
  • snorkelling observations;
  • visibility reports;
  • reviews;
  • descriptions;
  • comments;
  • photographs;
  • images;
  • videos;
  • GPS or geographical information;
  • information about where you have snorkelled; and
  • other information voluntarily submitted to SnorkelMap.

Much of this information is intended to be publicly available.

3.3 Location information

SnorkelMap is fundamentally a geographical service.

If you contribute a snorkel location, GPS position or other geographical information, that information may be displayed publicly on maps and elsewhere throughout SnorkelMap.

Snorkel location contributions are intended to form a persistent public record and, subject to applicable legal obligations, may remain publicly available indefinitely.

3.4 Images, videos and EXIF metadata

When you upload an image or video, technical information associated with the file may also be processed.

For photographs, this may include:

  • GPS coordinates;
  • date and time;
  • camera or device information;
  • orientation;
  • technical camera information; and
  • other metadata embedded in the file.

You are responsible for checking the information contained in an image or video before uploading it.

4. Information collected automatically

When you visit SnorkelMap, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address;
  • browser type and version;
  • operating system;
  • device information;
  • approximate geographical information derived from technical information;
  • pages or resources requested;
  • timestamps;
  • referring website;
  • technical logs;
  • security-related information;
  • information concerning interactions with the Website; and
  • information relating to errors, abuse or attempted attacks.

5. Cloudflare

SnorkelMap uses services provided by Cloudflare, including infrastructure and security services such as content delivery, caching, DDoS protection, Cloudflare R2 storage, Cloudflare Images and image transformation, Cloudflare Turnstile, and analytics or technical measurement services where enabled.

Cloudflare may process technical information, including IP addresses and other information transmitted through its infrastructure, as necessary to provide these services.

Cloudflare is used primarily to help protect SnorkelMap from malicious traffic, attacks, abuse and other security threats, and to improve the delivery and reliability of the Website.

Cloudflare is an independent service provider and may process information in accordance with its own privacy documentation and contractual arrangements with the charity.

Where applicable, SnorkelMap will implement appropriate measures required by UK data-protection and electronic-privacy law in relation to analytics and similar technologies.

6. Map services

SnorkelMap uses mapping technology, including Mapbox, to display geographical information and maps.

Mapbox may receive technical information and information necessary to provide mapping functionality.

7. Cookies

SnorkelMap uses cookies and similar technologies primarily for functionality, authentication and security.

For example, we may use a cookie to enable the “Remember me” login functionality.

Cookies may also be used where technically necessary to maintain a secure login session, authenticate users, protect accounts, prevent abuse, and maintain essential Website functionality.

We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

If we introduce non-essential analytics, advertising, tracking or other technologies in the future, we will review whether consent or other controls are required under applicable law and will update this Privacy Policy and our cookie arrangements accordingly.

8. Why we use personal information

We may use personal information to:

  • create and manage accounts;
  • authenticate users;
  • provide SnorkelMap functionality;
  • display user contributions;
  • maintain public snorkel location records;
  • allow users to edit their contributions;
  • operate maps and geographical features;
  • store and display images and videos;
  • communicate with users about their accounts;
  • provide support;
  • maintain the Website;
  • protect SnorkelMap from attacks;
  • detect and prevent malicious activity;
  • investigate abuse;
  • prevent fraud;
  • protect users and the charity;
  • maintain the security and integrity of the Website;
  • diagnose technical problems;
  • support marine conservation;
  • support scientific and educational purposes;
  • understand how the service is used;
  • communicate with users;
  • defend legal claims; and
  • administer the charity and its digital services.

The charity may use information contributed to SnorkelMap to support marine conservation, marine science, environmental education, research, understanding snorkelling environments, identifying environmental changes and improving knowledge of marine habitats.

9. Future analysis of images and other media

In the future, the charity may analyse images, videos and other user-contributed material using automated techniques, including artificial intelligence or machine-learning technologies, where this is reasonably connected to marine conservation, scientific, educational or environmental purposes.

Such analysis will not be undertaken for the purpose of selling users' personal data.

The charity does not currently intend to use user-contributed imagery for unrelated commercial advertising purposes.

Any future processing will remain subject to applicable data-protection law and this Privacy Policy may be updated where appropriate.

10. Our lawful bases for processing

Depending on the circumstances, we may rely on one or more of the following lawful bases under the UK GDPR:

Contract: where processing is necessary to provide an account or functionality that you have requested.

Legitimate interests: including operating and improving SnorkelMap, protecting the Website, preventing abuse and attacks, maintaining a reliable service, supporting marine conservation, scientific and educational purposes, maintaining a public record of snorkelling locations, understanding use, communicating with users, defending legal claims and administering the charity's digital services.

Legal obligation: where necessary to comply with a legal or regulatory obligation.

Consent: where consent is required by law.

Our lawful basis may vary depending on the particular processing activity.

11. Public information and user contributions

SnorkelMap is a community-generated service.

Users contribute information for the benefit of the wider community.

Information submitted as public SnorkelMap content may be visible to anyone on the internet, including people who do not have an account.

This may include usernames, snorkel location information, geographical coordinates, reviews, descriptions, comments, visibility reports, photographs, videos, dates, observations and other information voluntarily included by the contributor.

You should therefore treat public contributions as public information.

12. Persistent snorkel location records

An important feature of SnorkelMap is that user-contributed snorkel locations are intended to form a long-term public knowledge base.

A snorkel location listing, once contributed, may remain on SnorkelMap indefinitely, including after the contributing user's account has been deleted.

If a user requests account deletion, we will make a reasonable attempt to remove or anonymise the public association between their account and their contributions.

For example, where reasonably possible, a username may be replaced with:

“SnorkelMap User”

The underlying snorkel location information may nevertheless remain publicly available.

This approach is subject to applicable data-protection law.

13. Account deletion

You may request deletion of your SnorkelMap account by contacting:

snorkelmap@snorkellingsociety.org

Please use the subject line Account deletion request.

When an account is deleted, account credentials will be deleted or disabled, personal account information will be deleted or anonymised where appropriate, and the public association between your account and your contributions will, where reasonably possible, be anonymised.

Public SnorkelMap contributions may remain available.

We may retain information where we have a legitimate or legal reason to do so.

14. Retention of information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Public snorkel location information may be retained indefinitely because long-term preservation is a fundamental feature of the SnorkelMap service.

15. Who we share information with

We do not sell user data.

We may share or make information available to organisations that provide services necessary to operate SnorkelMap, including cloud infrastructure, hosting, storage, content delivery, DDoS protection, cybersecurity, image processing, authentication, mapping, communications, technical monitoring, analytics and other essential Website services.

Examples include Cloudflare and Mapbox.

We may also disclose information where necessary to comply with law, protect someone from serious harm, investigate fraud or abuse, protect the charity's rights, enforce our Terms and Conditions, or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

16. International transfers

The Snorkelling Society operates in the UK, but SnorkelMap is an internet service and some technology providers may operate internationally.

Your information may therefore be processed outside the UK.

Where UK data-protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we will seek to use an appropriate lawful mechanism and safeguards.

The SnorkelMap primary database is hosted in Germany, within the European Union.

17. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted connections, secure authentication, password hashing, access controls, infrastructure security, DDoS protection, security monitoring, backups and abuse prevention.

However, no internet service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

18. Passwords

You are responsible for keeping your SnorkelMap password confidential.

You must not share your password, use another person's account, attempt to obtain another person's credentials, or knowingly permit another person to use your account.

If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us as soon as possible.

19. Information concerning other people

You should avoid uploading photographs or videos containing identifiable people unless you have a proper basis for doing so.

Responsibility for obtaining any necessary permissions rests with the uploader.

Users should take particular care when uploading images of children or vulnerable people.

20. Special categories of personal data

Please do not intentionally submit sensitive personal information to SnorkelMap unless it is genuinely necessary.

SnorkelMap is not designed to collect such information.

21. Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable lawful basis, you may have rights under UK data-protection law, including rights to:

  • request access to personal information;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request erasure;
  • request restriction of processing;
  • object to certain processing;
  • request data portability;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • complain to the ICO.

These rights are not absolute and may be subject to legal exceptions.

22. Making a privacy request

Privacy requests should be sent to:

contact@snorkellingsociety.org

Account deletion requests should be sent to:

snorkelmap@snorkellingsociety.org

We may request information necessary to verify your identity before acting on a request.

23. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we use your personal information, please contact:

contact@snorkellingsociety.org

We encourage you to contact us first so that we can try to resolve the issue.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

24. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time because of changes to SnorkelMap, technology, service providers, applicable law, ICO guidance or our processing activities.

The latest version will be published on SnorkelMap.com.

25. Contact

For general privacy enquiries: contact@snorkellingsociety.org

For account deletion: snorkelmap@snorkellingsociety.org

For copyright complaints: contact@snorkellingsociety.org

Please use the subject line Copyright complaint.
